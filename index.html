THE GETAWAY MISSION

Original Story: You are the Underground Syndicate's most sought-after wheelman in Smash Bandits Runner. Sheriff Vance and the Highway Patrol have locked down the county borders. Smash contraband drops, collect bounties, and outrun the law!

🖥️ Desktop Controls (Mouse-Driven Steering)

  • Mouse Movement: Steer car smoothly towards cursor.
  • Right Mouse Button (Hold): Throttle / Nitro acceleration boost.
  • Automatic Forward Drive: Car cruises forward automatically!
  • Optional Keyboard: W/S for boost/brake, A/D or Arrows to steer, Space to handbrake/drift.
  • Abilities: 1/Q (Titan Tank), 2/E (Oil Slick), 3/R (Electric Shock). Each has an independent 150s cooldown.
  • Auto Reverse: Automatically backs out if stuck against a wall for ~2 seconds.
  • Pause: Press Esc or P.

📱 Mobile Controls

  • Virtual Joystick (Left Side): Touch & drag on the left half of the screen to steer smoothly. Push up for nitro boost, pull down for fast reverse braking!
  • Direct Touch-To-Steer: Tap or drag anywhere on screen to point your car directly towards your finger (just like the PC mouse cursor).
  • Right Buttons: Hold ⚡ BOOST for nitro speed, 🛑 REVERSE for immediate braking & swift backward escape, and 🏎️ DRIFT for handbrake turns.
  • Abilities (Bottom): Tap 1 Tank, 2 Oil Slick, or 3 Electric Shock to trigger special powers.

💥 Destruction & Damage

Smashing wooden fences, crates, fuel barrels, road signs, and roadside structures awards cash and builds your COMBO MULTIPLIER. Watch your VEHICLE HEALTH bar—crashing into solid walls at high speed causes heavy damage!
