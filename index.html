THE GETAWAY MISSION

Original Story: You are the Underground Syndicate's most sought-after wheelman. The corrupt County Task Force has locked down the county borders. Your mission: tear through the roadblocks, smash contraband drops for dirty cash, and escape Sheriff Vance's relentless pursuit cruisers.

🖥️ PC & Laptop Controls (Mouse Only Steering)

  • Mouse Movement: Steer car smoothly towards cursor.
  • Right Mouse Button (Hold): Throttle / Nitro boost.
  • Forward Drive: Car moves forward automatically!
  • Optional Keyboard: W/S for boost/brake, A/D or Arrows to steer, Space to handbrake/drift.
  • Abilities: Press 1 / Q (Titan Tank), 2 / E (Oil Slick), 3 / R (EMP Shock).
  • Pause: Press Esc or P.

📱 Mobile & Tablet Controls (One Circular Ring)

  • Circular Touch Ring (Bottom-Right): Touch and drag left or right to steer smoothly.
  • Automatic Drive: The vehicle cruises forward automatically without needing to hold a gas button.
  • Abilities (Bottom-Left): Tap the Tank, Oil Slick, or EMP icons with your left thumb.

💥 Cash & Smashing

Smash wooden fences, crates, fuel barrels, road signs, and roadside stalls to earn instant cash! Smashing objects quickly builds your COMBO MULTIPLIER for bonus payouts!
